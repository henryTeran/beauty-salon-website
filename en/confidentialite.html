01In brief

María Fernanda Beauté & Bien-être collects only the data needed to arrange your appointments. It is never sold or used for advertising. No audience measurement tool is activated without your consent. This policy follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP, as in force since 1 September 2023, known as the “nFADP”).

02Data controller

María Fernanda Rodriguez, María Fernanda Beauté & Bien-être, Geneva (Switzerland).

For any question or request about your data: rodmafer6@gmail.com · +41 78 689 80 20.

03Data processed

Depending on how you use the website, the following data may be processed:

  • Appointment request: full name, phone number, email address (optional), address for the home treatment, treatments chosen, date and time, free-text message and website language used.
  • Direct communication: the content of messages you send by WhatsApp, email or phone.
  • Technical data: IP address, date and time and browser type, recorded in the hosting provider’s logs for security purposes.
  • Audience measurement, only with your consent: pages viewed, device and approximate origin, in statistical form.

04Sensitive data

Some information useful for a treatment (pregnancy, allergies, skin conditions, contraindications) may be sensitive health data. Share only what is necessary: by providing it, you expressly consent to its use to adapt the treatment. It is never passed on to third parties.

05Purposes

  • To handle your request, confirm, change or cancel an appointment and contact you about it.
  • To show other visitors which time slots are already taken, without any data that identifies you.
  • To keep client records and meet legal obligations (accounting).
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.
  • Only with your consent: to compile visitor statistics.

06Basis for processing

Data is processed to prepare and provide the service you request, on the basis of your consent where required (sensitive data, audience measurement), an overriding interest (website security) or a legal obligation.

07Recipients and processors

Only the beautician has access to your data. It is stored or transmitted by the following providers, which act solely on instruction:

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): storage of appointment requests and the diary, technical sending of emails.
  • Google (Gmail): delivery of confirmation emails.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited): when you choose to send your request by WhatsApp, under that service’s terms.
  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages): hosting of the website pages.
  • Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited), only if you accept it: visitor statistics.

08Transfers abroad

These providers may process data outside Switzerland, in particular in the European Union and European Economic Area (recognised by Switzerland as providing adequate protection) and in the United States. In the latter case, the transfer relies on the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or otherwise on standard contractual clauses.

09Retention periods

  • Requests and appointments: 24 months after the last appointment, then deleted.
  • Accounting records: 10 years, in accordance with Swiss law.
  • Technical logs: for the period set by the hosting provider.
  • Cookie preference: 12 months on your device.

10Security

The website is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to the diary is protected by a personal account; strict access rules prevent any visitor from viewing another person’s data. Only the booked time slot is publicly visible. In the event of a data security breach posing a high risk, the Federal Commissioner and the persons concerned would be informed as required by law.

11Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses no advertising cookies and no social media trackers. On your first visit, a banner lets you accept or refuse optional items; you can change your choice at any time using the “Manage cookies” link at the bottom of the page.

  • Necessary (always active): remembering your cookie choice and making the booking work. They are kept in your browser’s local storage and are not used to track you.
  • Audience measurement (optional, off by default): Google Analytics, with shortened IP address, to see which pages are viewed most. “_ga” cookies, maximum lifetime 13 months. They are set only after you accept and deleted if you withdraw your consent.

12Your rights

At any time and free of charge you may:

  • ask which data about you is processed and obtain a copy (right of access and data portability);
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • ask for your data to be erased or object to its processing, within the limits of legal retention obligations;
  • withdraw consent you have given, without retroactive effect.

13Exercising your rights

Send your request to rodmafer6@gmail.com · +41 78 689 80 20. You will receive a reply within 30 days. You may also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), Feldeggweg 1, 3003 Bern — www.edoeb.admin.ch.

14Minors

Appointment requests concerning a minor must be made by their legal representative or with that person’s consent.

15Changes

This policy may be updated if the website or the legal framework changes. The version published on this page applies; if language versions differ, the French version prevails.
